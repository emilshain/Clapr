KLING 3.0 PROMPTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KEY PRINCIPLES:
  • Write like a Director of Photography, not a photographer
  • Describe MOTION and TIME explicitly — how things move, not just how they look
  • Use specific camera terminology: dolly, push, track, crane, tilt, pan, whip
  • Include lighting sources and mood
  • One clear action per shot — don't overload
  • Use @ref tokens to anchor characters/subjects

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KLING EXAMPLE 1: Establishing / Wide Shot
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Corporate lobby at sunrise

"Wide establishing shot of a modernist corporate lobby at dawn. Floor-to-ceiling
windows flood the space with golden sunlight. @lead_ref_01 enters from the left
in a sharp dark suit, walking purposefully toward the reception desk. The camera
slowly pushes in as he approaches, shallow depth of field blurring the elegant
seating area behind. Soft warm light plays across glass surfaces. Shot on 35mm
with minimal color grading, cool morning blue tones transitioning to warm amber."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KLING EXAMPLE 2: Close-Up with Dialogue / Emotion
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: High-pressure moment

"Extreme close-up of @lead_ref_01's face in shadow, eyes locked on something
off-camera. A moment of hesitation, then determination. Subtle head tilt, slight
jaw clench. The camera pulls back slowly, revealing he's standing in a rain-soaked
alley at night. Neon signs reflect in puddles. Cold blue and magenta lighting.
Film noir aesthetic, grainy 16mm look. One beat of silence, then dialogue begins."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KLING EXAMPLE 3: Action / Complex Motion
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Chase sequence

"Wide shot, @lead_ref_01 runs down a crowded Tokyo street at midday. Busy foot
traffic creates dynamic obstacles. The camera tracks left-to-right in a fast
handheld following shot, weaving between people. Bright daylight, saturated
colors, crowds in motion blur. Building energy. Cut to: tight profile close-up,
@lead_ref_01's face breathing hard, eyes forward, neon glow hitting one side.
Fast pace, documentary-style realism. All handheld, 24fps for slight motion blur."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KLING EXAMPLE 4: Multi-Shot Sequence
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Product demo sequence (6 shots in one generation)

"Shot 1: Wide overhead view of a walnut desk, pristine white background.
@product_ref_01 centered, morning light.
| Shot 2: Medium shot, @lead_ref_01's hands opening the product, slow deliberate
motion, focus pulls to product detail.
| Shot 3: Insert close-up, fingers running across brushed metal surface, tactile,
soft warm light.
| Shot 4: Medium shot, @lead_ref_01 looking up with slight smile, product held up
to light, confidence in eyes.
| Shot 5: Wide, @lead_ref_01 walks to window with product, golden hour light
floods in, silhouette. Slow dolly out.
| Shot 6: Tight close-up, product's screen glowing, reflection on @lead_ref_01's
face. Subtle head nod. End on product detail."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KLING EXAMPLE 5: Cinematic Hero Shot
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Establishing a character

"Slow dolly push-in on @lead_ref_01's face as sweat beads down his forehead. Eyes
intensely focused on something beyond the frame. Shallow depth of field, warm
color grade with high contrast. Soft practical light sources frame the shot.
Camera moves from wide to extreme close-up of eyes over 8 seconds. Cinematic,
dramatic, 2.39:1 aspect ratio feel. Film grain. Zero dialogue, pure visual story."

SEEDANCE 2.0 PROMPTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KEY PRINCIPLES:
  • Audio is FIRST — describe sound source, rhythm, and how it anchors the scene
  • One clear action per shot — break complex ideas across multiple shots
  • Use @Audio @Video @Image reference tags in your prompt IF using them
  • Keep sentences SHORT (5–10 words per line max)
  • Include FEELING and MOOD over technical detail
  • Duration MUST be: 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, or 15 seconds only
  • Describe surface and material for Foley (wet cobblestone vs. hardwood)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SEEDANCE EXAMPLE 1: Audio-First / Emotional Close-Up
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Intimate moment with piano (8 seconds)

"Soft piano music, slow contemplative.
Close-up of hands on black keys.
Shallow depth of field, warm amber lighting.
Fingers move gently, each note deliberate.
Camera pulls back slowly over the shoulders to reveal
@lead_ref_01 at the piano, expression focused but dreamy.
Painterly texture, film grain, muted warm color palette.
Emotional, introspective, jazz club aesthetic."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SEEDANCE EXAMPLE 2: Sound Design / Ambient Atmosphere
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Rain on glass, contemplative mood (6 seconds)

"Sound of heavy rain pattering on glass.
Soft ambient synth in background, melancholic.
Macro shot of raindrops sliding down a window.
Blurred lights outside create bokeh.
Warm interior light, cool blue tone from rain-soaked outside.
Camera slowly zooms in on one droplet falling.
Dreamy, slow-motion feeling, painterly grain.
Quiet, introspective mood."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SEEDANCE EXAMPLE 3: Stylized Character Performance
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Emotional monologue with music (10 seconds)

"Cinematic orchestral swell building slowly.
Medium close-up of @lead_ref_01, eyes glistening.
Soft directional key light from side, dramatic shadows.
Character delivers emotion through expression, minimal head movement.
Depth of field blurs abstract background.
Color grading: cool tones with warm face lighting.
Camera slight push-in, building intensity with music.
End on tight close-up of eyes. Powerful, cinematic, artistic."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SEEDANCE EXAMPLE 4: Multi-Shot with Audio Sync
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Stylized narrative sequence (15 seconds)

"Upbeat indie song, energetic, playful rhythm.
Shot 1 (4s): @lead_ref_01 laughing, twirling in sunlit room. Warm golden light.
Cut to Shot 2 (4s): Hands snapping, synchronizing with bass drop. Macro close-up.
Cut to Shot 3 (4s): Wide shot, @lead_ref_01 dancing freely, silhouette against
window, blue-hour light, joy in every motion.
Cut to Shot 4 (3s): Return to close-up, beaming smile, eye contact with camera.
Music peaks. Celebration mood, stylized, dreamy cinematography, soft-focus edges."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SEEDANCE EXAMPLE 5: Atmospheric Product Moment
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Product reveal with ambient sound (6 seconds)

"Gentle ambient droning sound, serene and minimal.
Hands gently unboxing @product_ref_01, reverent, slow motion.
Soft warm light hits product's brushed surface.
Close-up of fingers tracing edges.
Camera pulls back to reveal product glowing softly on wood surface.
Shallow bokeh, muted color palette, luxury feeling.
Peace and calm, tactile, premium aesthetic."

WAN / VAN 2.7 PROMPTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KEY PRINCIPLES:
  • Write as a documentary director — OBSERVE, don't direct
  • Ground everything in REALISM and specificity
  • Describe surfaces, materials, light SOURCE clearly
  • Include concrete sensory details: textures, colors, lighting conditions
  • For explicit content: describe clearly without ambiguity
  • Use camera anchors (reference images) for consistency
  • One sequence per prompt — clear beginning, middle, end

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WAN EXAMPLE 1: Corporate / Documentary
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Product presentation (8 seconds)

"Documentary-style wide shot of a bright modern office. @lead_ref_01 in business
casual stands at a glass table, holding @product_ref_01. Natural window light
illuminates the left side of his face. He demonstrates the product with calm
confidence, turning it slowly to show details. The camera holds wide, then gentle
push to medium. Realistic color grade, natural skin tones, sharp focus on product
details. Shot on 24fps, stable tripod. Professional, credible, trustworthy."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WAN EXAMPLE 2: Cinematic Realism / Everyday Drama
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Founder signing moment (6 seconds)

"Tight close-up of @lead_ref_01's hands as they sign a document on a walnut desk.
Fountain pen moving across paper with deliberate strokes. The signature is clear
and confident. Natural window light from above creates sharp shadows across the
desk surface. Coffee mug blurred in background, real office setting. Camera is
static, handheld slight micro-movements. Warm earth tones, photorealistic detail,
subtle dust particles in light. Tension and resolution. Shot on 35mm aesthetic."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WAN EXAMPLE 3: Interview Setup
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: One-on-one conversation (10 seconds)

"Medium shot of @lead_ref_01 sitting in a comfortable chair, against a neutral
soft-focus background wall. Professional three-point lighting: key light from
upper left, soft fill from right, subtle hair light. Natural color palette,
professional broadcast quality. @lead_ref_01 begins to speak, calm and poised,
occasionally gesturing. Light catches texture of fabric. Natural skin tones,
sharp on eyes. No movement — locked static frame. Documentary interview style,
credible and authentic."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WAN EXAMPLE 4: Architectural / Location Showcase
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Office walkthrough (12 seconds)

"Wide shot of a minimalist office space with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking
a city skyline. Morning light floods in. @lead_ref_01 walks across the space,
hand trailing along a polished wooden counter. The camera tracks smoothly from
left to right, revealing desks, plants, and natural materials. Warm light plays
on surfaces. Color-graded with lifted blacks and warm whites. Architectural
detail is clear: wood grain, glass reflections, concrete floors. Calm, aspirational,
realistic. Shot on Arri cinema camera aesthetic."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WAN EXAMPLE 5: Explicit/NSFW Content
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Scene: Adult scenario (description intentionally clear and specific)

"Close-up shot of two adults in an intimate moment. Clear focus on bodies in
contact, specific sexual activity visible and unambiguous. Natural indoor light
from bedside lamp, warm amber tones. Realistic skin texture and anatomical detail.
Heavy breathing and soft sound design. Camera is close and steady. Cinematic but
explicit. [Model: Van/WAN] is appropriate for this content; Kling or Seedance
would be censored."

SOUL CINEMA PROMPTS (Image Generation for Characters)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KEY PRINCIPLES:
  • Keep prompts under 100 words for best consistency
  • Use format: Age + Role/Profession + Physical details + Mood + Environment
  • Include distinctive marks: scars, tattoos, glasses, hairstyle
  • Specify pose or stance for cinematic quality
  • Lighting is crucial: "dramatic side light", "soft fill light", "neon glow"
  • End with cinematic style descriptor
  • Generate 3 variations and select strongest for Soul ID

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"28-year-old female tech founder. Sharp cheekbones, confident expression.
Dark hair pulled back sleekly. Wearing minimalist black turtleneck. Seated
at a modern glass desk. Key light from upper left creates strong shadows,
professional boardroom aesthetic. Shallow depth of field, blurred office
background. Cinematic photoreal, dramatic lighting, intense expression.
Corporate power aesthetic."

light playing across face. Cyberpunk cinematic, hacking terminal glow,

GOOGLE GEMINI PRO PROMPTS (Image Generation for Locations & Props)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KEY PRINCIPLES:
  • Focus on ENVIRONMENT and CONTEXT, not characters
  • Be specific: materials, surfaces, lighting sources, spatial relationships
  • Describe SCALE and PROPORTION clearly
  • Use architectural and design language
  • Include lighting direction and color temperature
  • Specify depth: foreground, midground, background layers
  • Keep it PHOTOREALISTIC unless stylization needed

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GEMINI EXAMPLE 1: Corporate Location
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"Modernist corporate lobby at dawn. Floor-to-ceiling windows with soft golden
sunlight streaming in. Polished concrete floors reflecting light. Minimalist
furniture: sleek reception desk in walnut wood, contemporary seating in neutral
linen. Potted fiddle leaf figs add subtle green. Architectural detail: steel
columns, exposed ceiling grid, skylights. Cool morning light from outside
contrasts with warm interior spots. Photorealistic, sharp focus, premium
aesthetics. Photography style: architectural documentary."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GEMINI EXAMPLE 2: Detailed Prop / Product Environment
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"Close-up product photography setup. Brushed stainless steel device mounted on
a white seamless backdrop. Soft box lighting from upper left creates smooth
highlights on curved surfaces. Shadow detail on right side. Shallow depth of
field. Wooden platform beneath product, natural grain texture visible.
Overhead view. Studio photography, museum quality lighting, commercial
product photography style. Sharp on product, soft background blur."

PROMPT WRITING CHECKLIST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FOR ALL MODELS:
  ☐ One clear PRIMARY action or subject
  ☐ Lighting SOURCE is named (window, lamp, neon, etc.)
  ☐ Camera MOVEMENT or ANGLE specified (if any)
  ☐ MOOD or ATMOSPHERE is clear
  ☐ Time of day (if relevant): dawn, noon, golden hour, night, etc.
  ☐ Color palette or tone is described

FOR KLING:
  ☐ Shot size clearly named (Wide, Medium, Close-Up, Insert)
  ☐ Motion verb is CINEMATIC (dolly, push, track, crane, not just "zoom")
  ☐ @ref tokens used for character/subject anchoring
  ☐ Aspect ratio or film format mentioned (optional but useful)
  ☐ Negative aspects avoided: "no text overlays", "natural motion", etc.

FOR SEEDANCE:
  ☐ AUDIO described first (sound source, mood, music)
  ☐ Duration snapped to valid value (4/5/6/8/10/12/15 only)
  ☐ Sentences kept SHORT (max 10 words per line)
  ☐ EMOTION and FEELING emphasized over technical detail
  ☐ Surface materials named for Foley (wet cobblestone, hardwood, glass)

FOR WAN/VAN:
  ☐ DOCUMENTARY tone — observe, don't direct
  ☐ Specific MATERIALS and TEXTURES described
  ☐ Light SOURCE detailed (natural window, lamp type, time of day)
  ☐ REALISTIC proportions and spatial relationship
  ☐ (If explicit) Content described clearly and specifically

FOR SOUL CINEMA:
  ☐ Under 100 words total
  ☐ Age + Role/Profession + Distinctive features
  ☐ Mood or expression included
  ☐ LIGHTING style explicitly named
  ☐ Final style descriptor: "Cinematic photoreal", "Fantasy aesthetic", etc.

FOR GEMINI PRO:
  ☐ LOCATION or ENVIRONMENT focus (not character face)
  ☐ Spatial layers clear: foreground, midground, background
  ☐ MATERIALS and SURFACES described (wood grain, concrete, glass)
  ☐ Scale and PROPORTION are specific
  ☐ Lighting direction FROM source to surface
